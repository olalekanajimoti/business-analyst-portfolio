## User-story format

> As a **[user]**, I need **[capability]**, so that **[business/user value]**.

## Quality checklist

- Unambiguous and testable
- Linked to a business objective
- Agreed owner and source
- Priority recorded
- Dependencies and constraints visible
- Acceptance criterion defined
- Validation evidence retained

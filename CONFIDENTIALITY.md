# Confidentiality and Evidence Statement

This repository contains retrospective case studies based on genuine professional and entrepreneurial experience.

To protect organisations, customers and collaborators:

- personal data and credentials are excluded;
- commercially sensitive values are omitted or generalised;
- internal screenshots are not published without permission;
- workflows are reconstructed to demonstrate analytical reasoning;
- requirements reflect the business need and delivered or proposed scope known to the author;
- product concepts are labelled separately from delivered client work;
- stated performance outcomes are included only where previously recorded by the organisation or project owner.

The purpose is to demonstrate Business Analyst capability, not to disclose confidential information or imply that reconstructed documents were the exact internal templates used at the time.
